01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Daily Goal Tracker Android application (the “App”), published by CREST HEALTH (PRIVATE) LIMITED (“we”, “us” or “our”).

The App helps you set daily goals that you can count (for example “Read 20 pages”) or simply mark as done, log your progress, build streaks, unlock achievement badges and review your progress in charts, a history calendar and insights. A new day starts at midnight: your progress resets and the finished day is saved to your history.

The App is designed to work entirely offline. We do not operate any servers for the App, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information.

02 Information We Collect

Personal information

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or any other identifying details, and it has no sign-in or account system.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following information in its private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else by the App.

Device and non-personal information

The App does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, usage statistics or crash reports, and it does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

03 How Information Is Used

The information stored on your device is used only to provide the App's features, on your device:

  • Displaying your goals and today's progress.
  • Saving each finished day to your history and resetting progress when a new day starts.
  • Calculating streaks, completion rates and the charts shown in Insights and History.
  • Deciding when an achievement badge should unlock.
  • Remembering your settings, such as the theme.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any automated decision-making.

04 App Permissions

The App does not request any device permissions. It cannot access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or notifications, and it does not have the Internet permission.

05 Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, payments, cloud storage or AI, and it does not use Firebase or any other online service.

It is built only with Google's Android Jetpack (AndroidX) and Material Components libraries and the Kotlin standard libraries, which run on your device and do not collect data.

Links that open other apps

Some options in Settings hand you over to another app. Once you leave the App, that app's own privacy policy applies:

  • Rate Us opens the App's page in the Google Play Store app or in your browser.
  • Share App opens your device's share menu with a short message and a link to the App on Google Play. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.
  • Privacy Policy opens this page in your browser.

06 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. Because the App has no internet access and we run no servers, your goal data is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the App.

07 Android Backup & Device Transfer

The App supports Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on backup in your device settings, Android may include the App's data (your goals, history, badges and settings) in your device backup to your Google Account. It can also copy this data to a new phone when you transfer your data to it.

This feature is controlled by you in your device settings and handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to these backups.

08 Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed, or until you delete it. You can remove it at any time:

  • Clear All Data (Settings → Data) permanently deletes all your goals, history and daily summaries. Your settings and unlocked badges are kept.
  • Delete a goal by swiping it away on the Home screen.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes everything, including settings and badges.

Copies inside an Android device backup are kept and deleted under your Google Account settings, not by the App.

09 Data Security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage, which Android's app sandbox protects from other apps. Because the App never transmits your data over a network, it cannot be intercepted in transit or exposed through a server breach.

Keeping your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software helps keep this information safe.

10 Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, no information from children is received by us.

11 Your Rights & Choices

Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You stay in full control on your own device:

  • View and edit your goals at any time in the App.
  • Delete your goals and history with Clear All Data, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Turn Android backup on or off in your device settings.

If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us using the details below.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. If a future version of the App starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
